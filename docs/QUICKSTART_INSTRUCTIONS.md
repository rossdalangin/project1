# Quickstart Instructions & Site Owner Field Guide

Welcome to **Ross Portfolio Theme**! This guide walks you through setting up, customizing, and launching your new high-performance personal portfolio website in less than 10 minutes.

---

## Step 1: Install & Activate the Theme

1. Download `ross-portfolio-theme.zip`.
2. Go to your WordPress Admin Dashboard (`/wp-admin`).
3. Navigate to **Appearance > Themes**.
4. Click **Add New** > **Upload Theme**.
5. Select `ross-portfolio-theme.zip` and click **Install Now**.
6. Click **Activate**.

*Note: Upon activation, the built-in seeder will automatically set up your starter pages (Home, Blog, Contact, Projects, About), create a demo post, and set your static homepage.*

---

## Step 2: Personalize Your Profile in the Customizer

All content on your homepage and profile card can be customized without editing any code.

1. Navigate to **Appearance > Customize > Portfolio & Profile Settings**.
2. **Profile Details:**
   - Enter your **Display Name** and **Job Title / Role**.
   - Fill in your **Location** (e.g. `San Francisco, CA & Remote`), **Email Address**, and **Phone Number**.
   - Upload a square headshot into **Profile Avatar Photo** (512x512px WebP or PNG recommended).
   - Add your social profile URLs: **GitHub**, **LinkedIn**, and **Twitter / X**.
3. **Hero Section:**
   - Customize the **Hero Status Badge** (e.g., `Available for Architecture Advisory`).
   - Write your primary headline in **Hero Main Headline**.
   - Set up your CTA buttons (e.g., "Schedule Consultation" linking to `/contact/`).
4. **Key Metrics:**
   - Set up your three proof-points (e.g., `12+` Years Experience, `99.99%` Production Uptime, `10M+` Active Monthly Users).
5. **Skills & Capabilities:**
   - Customize your 6 core competencies with titles, descriptions, and built-in SVG icons.
6. **Technical FAQs:**
   - Add answers to common consulting questions (these automatically generate Google and AI search schemas!).
7. Click **Publish** to save your changes.

---

## Step 3: Select Your Color Preset

Want to change the accent colors? You can switch design palettes with one click:
1. In **Appearance > Customize > Portfolio & Profile Settings**, locate **Color Theme Preset**.
2. Choose from:
   - **Obsidian Cyber** (Electric Blue & Emerald accents - default)
   - **Midnight Violet** (Electric Violet & Neon Cyan)
   - **Emerald Matrix** (Terminal Mint & Cyber Cyan)
   - **Sunset Ember** (Coral Amber & Sunfire Gold)
   - **Royal Indigo** (Deep Indigo & Soft Blue)
   - **Minimal Titanium** (Sleek Slate & Crisp White)
3. Click **Publish**.

---

## Step 4: Verify the Contact Form

1. Navigate to your live site and visit the **/contact/** page.
2. Ensure the form displays your configured response time guarantee and contact details.
3. Submit a test message to ensure your WordPress server or SMTP provider delivers emails to your inbox.
   *(Tip: For production deliverability, make sure you have an SMTP plugin such as WP Mail SMTP configured).*

---

## Step 5: Publishing Case Studies and Articles

- **Blog Articles:** Go to **Posts > Add New** to draft articles. Featured images will automatically format with glassmorphic cards and responsive layouts.
- **Dedicated Blog Template:** If you prefer a two-column blog layout with a right-hand sidebar for widgets, create a page and choose the **Blog with Right Sidebar Template** under Page Attributes.

---

## Step 6: Verify Accessibility and Speed

- Test your keyboard navigation: Press the `Tab` key on your homepage. You will see the **Skip to main content** link appear at the top.
- Open the mobile navigation on your smartphone or browser responsive mode to test the drawer and focus trap.
- Run your URL through [Google PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/) to verify your near-perfect 100/100 Core Web Vitals score!
